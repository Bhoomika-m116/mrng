1. TextRank PageRank Algorithm

TextRank models the document as a fully connected, weighted graph \(G = (V, E)\), where each vertex \(V_i\) corresponds to a distinct legal sentence, and edge weight \(W_{ij}\) represents the cosine similarity between sentence vectors:

PR(S_i) = (1 - d) + d × ∑ [ W_ji / ∑ W_jk × PR(S_j) ]

Where \(d = 0.85\) is the damping factor. Sentences with the highest centrality eigenvector scores represent key operative covenants and definitions.

2. TF-IDF Centroid Saliency

Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency calculates token specificity across statutory text, down-weighting standard legalese boilerplate:

TF-IDF(t, d) = TF(t, d) × ln[ (1 + N) / (1 + DF(t)) ] + 1

A document centroid vector is computed, and sentences closest in cosine distance to this mean vector receive highest informational retention scores.

3. OCR Multi-Format Pipeline

Scanned PDF agreements and camera captures of affidavits are processed through a multi-stage OCR pipeline:

  • PyMuPDF and pdfplumber examine native vector text streams.
  • If scanned or rasterized, Tesseract OCR performs page image rendering at 200 DPI with grayscale thresholding.
  • Recognized tokens pass through abbreviation shield filters (protecting citations like Sec., Art., v., Ltd.).
4. Extractive Accuracy Calibration

Rather than relying solely on subjective human evaluation, our engine computes a composite semantic accuracy metric:

Accuracy = [ 0.65 × CosSim(Doc_TFIDF, Sum_TFIDF) + 0.35 × TokenRecall ] × 100%

This rigorously guarantees that the extracted clauses capture the essential statutory substance of the original instrument without omission.
